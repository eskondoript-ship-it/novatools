Privacy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

The short version: the tools run inside your browser. Text you type, images you drop in and files you convert are never sent to us, because there is no server here to send them to. This site is static files.

What the tools do with your data

Nothing leaves your device. Resizing an image, decoding a token, hashing a string, converting a file — all of it happens in the browser tab, using the browser's own APIs. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and every tool still works. That is the test.

What we store

No accounts, no logins, no database. Some tools remember a setting in your browser's own local storage so it is still there next visit; that never leaves your device and clearing your browser data removes it.

Advertising

This site shows ads to pay for itself. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and others.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of other vendors at aboutads.info/choices.

Analytics

If page analytics are in use, they count visits and which tools get opened. They are not linked to a name and cannot identify you.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13 and asks for no personal information from anyone.
